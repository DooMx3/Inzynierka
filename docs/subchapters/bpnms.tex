\subsection{BPNMs}
BPMN notation was used to ensure a thorough understanding and design of the key business processes taking place within the system. It enables a clear visual representation of task flows and interactions between the user, the system and the database \cite{white2004introduction}. Only chosen processes are described in this section. The selected processes were chosen based on their complexity and importance for the system.
