\section{Introduction}
\subsection{Purpose of work}

Our goal is to create an app that offers a simple and intuitive way to manage a~vineyard, in order to support producers and provide consumers with detailed information about the products they choose.

\subsection{Scope of work}
\begin{itemize}
	\item Analysis of existing market solutions.
	
	\item Design of the database architecture and UI.
	
	\item Implementation of the server-side and front-end components.
	
	\item Conducting performance and functional tests.
	
	\item Preparation of technical documentation and user manuals.
\end{itemize}
